Transportation

Getting to Ann Arbor

The closest major airport is Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport (DTW), approximately 45 minutes from Ann Arbor by car. Several ground transportation options are available from both terminals.

Rideshare
Uber & Lyft
McNamara Terminal: Level 4 of the McNamara Parking Garage — cross the skybridge from the terminal.

Evans Terminal: Level 1 of the Evans Parking Garage — cross the skybridge.

Collect baggage, follow Ground Transportation/Skybridge signs, and check your app for the exact pickup bay.
Taxi & Luxury Sedan
Metro Cars / Metro Cab
Officially serviced by Metro Cars and Metro Cab. Both dispatch from the Ground Transportation Center (Level 4 at McNamara; upper Level 4 at Evans).

Metro Cab: (734) 997-6500
Metro Cars: (800) 456-1701

On-site kiosks also available at both terminals.
Coach Bus — Recommended
Michigan Flyer / AirRide
Michigan Flyer offers direct nonstop motorcoach service between DTW and downtown Ann Arbor. Affordable and convenient — a great option for solo travelers. Boards from the Ground Transportation Centers at both terminals.
Express Bus
Detroit Air Xpress (DAX)
DAX provides nonstop express bus service to downtown Detroit. Pick up at Evans Terminal (Stalls 1–2) or McNamara Terminal (Departures Level, near Door 1).
Public Transit
SMART Bus
SMART Bus routes including FAST Michigan Route 261 and Route 125 serve both terminal Ground Transportation Centers, connecting DTW to Detroit and nearby communities.
Rental Car
On-Site Rental Shuttles
Off-site rental car courtesy shuttles pick up directly from the Ground Transportation Centers at both terminals. All major rental car companies are represented. A free inter-terminal shuttle also runs every 10 minutes between McNamara and Evans if needed.
